《最后一盏灯》隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 17 日

本政策说明《最后一盏灯》（以下称“本游戏”）收集哪些数据、怎么用、交给谁，以及你怎么管理自己的选择。本游戏由森澜岛游戏（以下称“我们”）提供。

1. 我们不收集的

  • 本游戏没有我们自己的账号注册，不要求你提供姓名、电话、邮箱或通讯录。本游戏使用 Google Play 游戏服务登录，见第 5 条。
  • 我们不收集精确位置，不读取你的照片、文件、麦克风或摄像头。
  • 游戏进度保存在你的设备上；如果你登录了 Google Play 游戏，进度还会通过 Google 的云存档保存（见第 5 条）。

2. 广告（Google AdMob）

本游戏含广告：小镇和结算页底部的横幅广告，以及你主动选择观看、看完可获得游戏内奖励的激励视频。激励视频只有你点了才会播放，是否观看由你决定，不看不影响主线进度。

为展示广告、统计广告效果和防止广告作弊，Google 移动广告 SDK 会自动收集并发送给 Google：

  • 设备标识符：Android 广告 ID、应用集 ID；
  • IP 地址（可用于推断大致位置，例如国家或城市）；
  • 与广告和应用的互动：应用启动、点击、视频观看；
  • 崩溃日志和诊断信息：应用崩溃记录、启动时间、卡顿率、耗电等性能数据。

这些数据由 Google 按其隐私政策处理：https://policies.google.com/privacy ；Google 如何使用合作伙伴应用中的数据：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. 统计（Google Analytics for Firebase）

为了解游戏怎么被使用、改进玩法和修复问题，Firebase 统计会收集：

  • 应用实例 ID（Firebase 为本次安装生成的随机 ID）；
  • 经过处理的 IP 地址（只用于推断大致位置）；
  • 设备型号、系统版本、语言等设备和应用信息；
  • 游戏内的使用情况：打开的界面、游戏时长、会话次数；
  • 应用内购买记录：商品 ID、商品名称和价格（不含任何付款卡信息）。

如果你的设备允许，Firebase 统计可能会读取 Android 广告 ID，并和其他统计数据一起用于统计分析。你可以在手机系统设置的广告选项中（例如「设置 → 隐私 → 广告」，因机型而异）重置或删除广告 ID。与你的设备相关联的统计数据按我们在 Firebase 中设置的保留期保存，最长不超过 14 个月。

4. 购买

游戏内购买由 Google Play 处理。银行卡等付款信息由 Google 收集和处理，我们拿不到。我们只会收到用于发放商品的订单结果。

5. Google Play 游戏服务（登录、排行榜、云存档）

  • 自动登录：如果你的设备已登录 Google Play 游戏，本游戏启动时会自动用它登录。Google 会把你的 Play 游戏玩家身份（玩家 ID、昵称和头像）提供给本游戏。
  • 排行榜：每周挑战的成绩会提交到 Google Play 游戏排行榜，其他玩家可能看到你的昵称、头像和分数，可见范围按你在 Play 游戏个人资料中的设置。
  • 云存档：你的游戏进度和购买恢复所需的记录会通过 Play 游戏云存档保存在 Google 的服务器上，换手机或重装后可以找回。
  • 玩家 ID 只在本游戏和 Google Play 游戏服务之间使用，不会发送到我们自己的服务器。
  • Play 游戏服务还会收集用于改进服务稳定性的统计和诊断数据，并使用 HTTPS 加密传输。这些数据由 Google 按其隐私政策处理：https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • 你的选择：你可以在 Play 游戏个人资料（https://play.google.com/games/profile ）或 Google 账号（https://myaccount.google.com ）中查看、修改公开范围，或删除你的 Play 游戏资料和本游戏的云端游戏数据。
  • 不使用 Play 游戏也可以玩本游戏，只是排行榜和云存档不可用。
  • 游戏内「设置 → 删除本机数据」只删除这台手机上的数据，不会删除云存档；要删云存档，请按上一条操作。

6. 应用内更新、远程内容、分享和本地通知

  • 应用内更新：本游戏使用 Google Play 应用内更新提示你有新版本。为判断是否有更新及更新大小，Google Play 商店会处理设备信息、应用版本等数据，按 Google Play 服务条款处理并加密传输。我们不会收到这些数据。
  • 远程内容和开关：本游戏会从 GitHub Pages 下载内容更新和功能开关（例如广告开关）。下载时，托管服务商 GitHub 会按其隐私声明处理 IP 地址等访问信息；这些下载不包含你的游戏数据或身份信息，我们也不会收到这些访问记录。
  • 分享：你主动点「分享」时，内容会交给你在手机上选择的应用发送，由那个应用按它的政策处理；本游戏不会自动替你分享。
  • 本地通知：本游戏可能在你的设备上发送本地提醒（例如每日补给）。通知在设备本地生成，不会为此把数据发出设备。你可以在手机「设置 → 应用 → 通知」中随时关闭。

6A. 欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士用户：同意与撤回

  • 首次打开游戏时，你会看到由 Google 用户消息平台（UMP）提供的同意窗口，可以选择同意或不同意。
  • 在你作出选择之前，我们不会请求广告。
  • 在你作出选择之后，按你的选择展示广告：同意的，可能展示个性化广告；不同意的，Google 可能只展示非个性化或有限的广告，或不展示广告。无论哪种，游戏本身照常可玩。
  • 即使是非个性化广告，Google 仍可能使用设备标识符和 Cookie / 本地存储来限制同一广告的展示次数、统计效果和防止作弊。
  • 随时撤回或修改：点小镇顶栏的「隐私设置」按钮，或游戏内「设置 → 广告隐私设置」，重新打开同意窗口修改你的选择。这两个入口只在需要征得同意的地区显示。
  • 你还可以在手机「设置 → 隐私 → 广告」中重置或删除广告 ID。

7. 删除数据请求

本节适用于 Google Play 上的游戏《最后一盏灯》（开发者：森澜岛游戏）。

怎么申请：用任意邮箱发邮件到 perngn88@gmail.com，标题写「删除数据请求 - 最后一盏灯」。正文写清：你的 Google Play 游戏玩家名称（如有），以及你想删除哪些数据。我们会在收到后 30 天内处理并回复。

你也可以自己马上删除：

  • 本机游戏数据：卸载游戏，或在 Android 设置里清除本应用的数据，即可删除保存在手机上的进度和设置。
  • 云端存档、排行榜成绩和 Play 游戏资料：打开「Google Play 游戏」应用，按第 5 条在设置里删除本游戏的数据。
  • 广告 ID：在手机「设置 → 隐私 → 广告」里重置或删除。

会删除的数据：本机游戏进度和设置、云端存档、排行榜成绩和 Play 游戏资料（按上面的方法删除；发邮件申请时，我们会告诉你每一步怎么删）。

会保留的数据：

  • 购买和订单记录：由 Google Play 保存，按 Google 的政策和法律要求（如税务、退款）保留，我们无法删除；你可以在 Google Play 的“付款和订阅”里查看。
  • 广告相关数据：由 Google AdMob 按 Google 的隐私政策处理，详见 https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites。
  • 游戏统计数据：不关联你的名字、邮箱或玩家名称，我们无法按玩家找到并单独删除；在统计服务设定的保留期（最长 14 个月）结束后自动删除。

购买记录的使用：游戏内购买由 Google Play 结算处理，我们看不到你的付款信息。统计服务只记录购买了哪个商品，用于了解游戏运营情况。

8. 数据安全

The Last Lamp — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what data The Last Lamp (the "Game") collects, how it is used, who receives it, and how you can manage your choices. The Game is provided by 森澜岛游戏 ("we", "us").

1. What we do not collect

  • The Game has no account sign-up of its own. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email or contacts. The Game uses Google Play Games Services sign-in; see Section 5.
  • We do not collect precise location, and we do not access your photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device; if you are signed in to Google Play Games, it is also saved through Google's cloud save (see Section 5).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game contains ads: a banner at the bottom of the town and results screens, and rewarded videos that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. Rewarded videos play only when you tap them; watching is optional and not required to progress the main story.

To serve ads, measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • IP address (which may be used to estimate general location, such as country or city);
  • Interactions with ads and the app: app launches, taps and video views;
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: app crash records, app launch time, hang rate, energy usage and other performance data.

Google processes this data under its Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the Game is used, improve gameplay and fix problems, Firebase Analytics collects:

  • App instance ID (a random ID Firebase creates for this installation);
  • A processed IP address (used only to estimate general location);
  • Device and app information such as device model, OS version and language;
  • In-game usage: screens viewed, play time and number of sessions;
  • In-app purchase records: product ID, product name and price (no payment card details).

Where your device allows it, Firebase Analytics may read the Android advertising ID and use it, together with other analytics data, for analytics. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's ad settings (for example, Settings → Privacy → Ads; this varies by device). Analytics data linked to your device is kept for the retention period we set in Firebase, for no longer than 14 months.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Payment card and other payment details are collected and processed by Google; we never receive them. We only receive the order result needed to deliver your item.

5. Google Play Games Services (sign-in, leaderboards, cloud save)

  • Automatic sign-in: if your device is signed in to Google Play Games, the Game signs you in automatically when it starts. Google provides your Play Games identity (player ID, gamer name and avatar) to the Game.
  • Leaderboards: weekly challenge scores are submitted to Google Play Games leaderboards. Other players may see your gamer name, avatar and score, depending on your Play Games profile settings.
  • Cloud save: your game progress and the records needed to restore purchases are saved on Google's servers through Play Games cloud save, so you can recover them on a new phone or after reinstalling.
  • Your player ID is used only between the Game and Google Play Games Services and is not sent to our own servers.
  • Play Games Services also collects analytics and diagnostics data to improve service stability, encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Google processes this data under its Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Your choices: you can review or change your profile visibility, or delete your Play Games profile and this game's cloud data, in your Play Games profile (https://play.google.com/games/profile) or your Google Account (https://myaccount.google.com).
  • You can play the Game without Play Games; leaderboards and cloud save will not be available.
  • "Settings → Delete data on this device" in the Game deletes data on this phone only and does not delete your cloud save; to delete cloud data, use the option above.

6. In-app updates, remote content, sharing and local notifications

  • In-app updates: the Game uses Google Play in-app updates to tell you when a new version is available. To check whether an update is available and how large it is, the Google Play Store processes data such as device information and app version, under the Google Play Terms of Service and encrypted in transit. We do not receive this data.
  • Remote content and switches: the Game downloads content updates and feature switches (for example, the ads switch) from GitHub Pages. When it does, the hosting provider GitHub processes access information such as your IP address under its privacy statement. These downloads do not include your game data or identity, and we do not receive these access logs.
  • Sharing: when you tap "Share", the content is handed to the app you choose on your phone, which handles it under its own policy. The Game never shares on your behalf automatically.
  • Local notifications: the Game may show local reminders on your device (for example, daily Supplies). They are created on your device and no data leaves your device for them. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's Settings → Apps → Notifications.

6A. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: consent and withdrawal

  • When you first open the Game, you will see a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where you can consent or decline.
  • We do not request any ads before you make your choice.
  • After you make your choice, ads follow your choice: if you consent, you may see personalized ads; if you decline, Google may show only non-personalized or limited ads, or no ads. Either way, the Game remains fully playable.
  • Even for non-personalized ads, Google may still use device identifiers and cookies/local storage for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Withdraw or change your choice at any time: tap the Privacy button in the town's top bar, or go to Settings → Ad privacy settings in the Game, to reopen the consent form. Both are shown only in regions where consent is required.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

7. Data Deletion Requests

This section applies to the game "最后一盏灯 (The Last Lamp)" on Google Play (developer: 森澜岛游戏).

How to request: Email perngn88@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request - The Last Lamp". Include your Google Play Games player name (if any) and which data you want deleted. We will process your request and reply within 30 days.

You can also delete some data yourself right away:

  • On-device game data: uninstall the game, or clear the app's data in Android settings, to delete progress and settings stored on your phone.
  • Cloud saves, leaderboard scores and your Play Games profile: open the Google Play Games app and delete this game's data in its settings, as described in Section 5.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Data that gets deleted: on-device progress and settings, cloud saves, leaderboard scores and your Play Games profile (using the steps above; if you email us, we will walk you through each step).

Data we keep:

  • Purchase and order records: kept by Google Play under Google's policies and legal requirements (such as tax and refunds). We cannot delete them. You can view them in Google Play under "Payments & subscriptions".
  • Ad-related data: handled by Google AdMob under Google's privacy policy. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Game analytics data: not linked to your name, email or player name, so we cannot find and delete it for a single player; it is deleted automatically when the analytics retention period ends (at most 14 months).

How purchase records are used: In-game purchases are processed by Google Play billing, and we cannot see your payment details. Our analytics only records which item was bought, to help us understand how the game is doing.

8. Security

All data collected by the SDKs and services above is encrypted in transit using TLS (HTTPS).

9. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
