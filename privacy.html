《最后一盏灯》隐私政策

开发者：森澜岛游戏 · 适用应用：最后一盏灯（Android，包名 com.lastlamp.game）· 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日

1. 我们自己不收集你的个人信息

《最后一盏灯》是一款单机游戏，没有我们自己的账号系统，也没有我们自己的服务器（排行榜用的是 Google Play 游戏，见第 3 条）。游戏进度（修好的房子、点亮的灯、碎片、飞机等级）和声音开关只保存在你手机上的这个应用里，我们看不到，也不会上传。

如果你自己点了游戏里的“分享”，游戏会在手机上生成一张你的小镇的图片和一段带链接的文字，交给你选择的应用（例如 Facebook、WhatsApp），发不发、发给谁由你决定；分享的内容只有游戏画面和游戏进度，不包含你的个人信息，之后由那个应用按它自己的隐私政策处理。我们的分享页面（nickooi.github.io/lastlamp/share/）只用来显示链接卡片并跳转到 Google Play，不统计访问、不放广告。

如果你的手机开启了 Android 系统备份，系统可能会把本应用的数据（包括游戏进度）备份到你自己的 Google 账号，这由 Google 按其政策管理。

2. 广告（Google AdMob）

游戏里只有横幅广告：小镇界面底部和每局打完的结算页底部各有一条小横幅；不弹全屏广告，也不需要看广告换奖励，战斗中没有广告。广告由 Google AdMob 提供。为了投放广告、统计广告效果和防止作弊，Google 移动广告 SDK 会自动收集并发送给 Google 以下信息：

  • 设备的 IP 地址（可能用于估算大致位置）；
  • 广告相关的使用情况，例如应用启动、点击、广告观看；
  • 诊断信息，例如应用和 SDK 的性能数据；
  • 设备标识符，例如 Android 广告 ID、应用组 ID。

这些数据在传输过程中经过加密，由 Google 按其隐私政策处理：https://policies.google.com/privacy，Google 如何使用合作伙伴应用中的数据：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites。

你可以在手机的 Android 设置里重置或删除广告 ID，也可以选择不接收个性化广告。

3. Google Play 游戏（排行榜）

游戏里有“守夜榜”和“每周挑战榜”两个排行榜，由 Google Play 游戏服务提供。如果你的手机登录了 Google Play 游戏，打开游戏时会自动登录（不需要另外注册）；没有 Play 游戏账号也可以照常玩，只是不上排行榜。登录后，游戏会把下面这些交给 Google Play 游戏：

  • 排行榜成绩：五个街区守到的最高夜数之和、每周挑战的成绩；
  • 你的 Play 游戏资料（昵称、头像、玩家 ID）会显示在排行榜上，谁能看到由你在 Play 游戏里的资料公开范围设置决定；
  • Google Play 游戏服务 SDK 自己收集的使用情况和诊断信息，用于改进它的稳定性。

这些数据由 Google 按其隐私政策处理（https://policies.google.com/privacy），传输过程中经过加密；我们看不到你的 Google 账号信息。你可以在 Play 游戏的资料设置里修改公开范围，或删除你的 Play 游戏资料和其中的游戏数据。

4. 欧洲等地区的广告同意

在法律要求征得同意的地区（例如欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士），游戏会通过 Google 的用户消息平台（UMP）询问你是否同意个性化广告。你可以随时在开始画面或小镇顶栏的“隐私设置”里修改这个选择。

5. 儿童

本游戏不以 13 岁以下儿童为目标用户。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

6. 删除数据

卸载游戏，或在 Android 设置里清除本应用的数据，就会删除手机上的全部游戏进度。广告相关数据由 Google 管理，你可以按上面第 2 条重置广告 ID；排行榜上的成绩和 Play 游戏资料按第 3 条在 Play 游戏里删除。

7. 政策更新和联系我们

政策有变化时，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。对本政策有任何问题，请通过 Google Play 商店本应用页面“开发者联系方式”里的邮箱联系我们。

The Last Lamp — Privacy Policy

Developer: 森澜岛游戏 · App: The Last Lamp (Android, package com.lastlamp.game) · Effective date: October 12, 2026

1. We do not collect your personal information

The Last Lamp is a single-player game with no accounts and no servers of our own (leaderboards use Google Play Games, see section 3). Your progress (repaired houses, lit lamps, shards, plane level) and your sound setting are stored only inside the app on your phone. We cannot see them and they are never uploaded to us.

If you tap "Share" in the game, the game creates a picture of your town and a short text with a link on your phone and hands them to the app you choose (for example Facebook or WhatsApp); you decide whether and to whom to send them. The shared content contains only game visuals and progress, no personal information, and is then handled by that app under its own privacy policy. Our share pages (nickooi.github.io/lastlamp/share/) only show the link preview and redirect to Google Play; they do not track visits or show ads.

If Android system backup is turned on, Android may back up this app's data (including your progress) to your own Google account, under Google's policies.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game only shows banner ads: a small banner at the bottom of the town screen and of the results screen after each battle. There are no pop-up full-screen ads, no ads to watch for rewards and no ads during battles. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate general location);
  • ad-related interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information such as app and SDK performance;
  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit and handled by Google under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy; how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Android settings.

3. Google Play Games (leaderboards)

The game has two leaderboards, "Night Watch" and "Weekly Challenge", provided by Google Play Games services. If your phone is signed in to Google Play Games, the game signs you in automatically when it starts (no separate registration); without a Play Games account you can still play, you just won't appear on the leaderboards. Once signed in, the game sends Google Play Games:

  • leaderboard scores: the sum of the highest night reached in the five districts, and your Weekly Challenge score;
  • your Play Games profile (gamer name, avatar, player ID) is shown on the leaderboards; who can see it depends on your Play Games profile visibility setting;
  • usage and diagnostic data collected by the Google Play Games services SDK itself to improve its stability.

This data is handled by Google under its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and encrypted in transit; we cannot see your Google account information. You can change your profile visibility, or delete your Play Games profile and its game data, in your Play Games profile settings.

4. Ad consent in Europe and other regions

Where consent is required by law (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent to personalized ads through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your choice at any time from “Privacy” (“隐私设置” in Chinese) on the title screen or in the town's top bar.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Deleting data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing the app's data in Android settings, deletes all game progress on your phone. Ad-related data is managed by Google; you can reset your advertising ID as described in section 2. Leaderboard scores and your Play Games profile can be deleted in Play Games as described in section 3.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date above. For any question about this policy, please email us at the address listed under “Developer contact” on this app's Google Play store page.
