《最后一盏灯》隐私政策

开发者：森澜岛游戏 · 适用应用：最后一盏灯（Android，包名 com.lastlamp.game）· 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日（6.7 版）

1. 我们没有自己的账号系统和服务器

《最后一盏灯》是一款单机游戏，没有我们自己的账号系统，也没有我们自己的服务器（排行榜和云端存档用的是 Google Play 游戏，见第 3、5 条；购买由 Google Play 处理，见第 4 条）。游戏进度（修好的房子、点亮的灯、碎片、飞机等级等）和声音开关保存在你手机上的这个应用里；如果你登录了 Google Play 游戏，进度还会存一份到你自己 Google 账号的云端（见第 5 条）。我们看不到这些，也不会上传给我们。为了改进游戏，游戏会把游戏统计发给 Google Analytics for Firebase，统计里没有你的名字和邮箱（见第 6 条）。

如果你自己点了游戏里的“分享”，游戏会在手机上生成一张你的小镇的图片和一段带链接的文字，交给你选择的应用（例如 Facebook、WhatsApp），发不发、发给谁由你决定；分享的内容只有游戏画面和游戏进度，不包含你的个人信息，之后由那个应用按它自己的隐私政策处理。我们的分享页面（nickooi.github.io/lastlamp/share/）只用来显示链接卡片并跳转到 Google Play，不统计访问、不放广告。

游戏会从我们放在 GitHub Pages（nickooi.github.io）上的网页下载游戏内容更新和开关配置（例如是否暂时关闭某个玩法，像祈愿）。这些请求只下载、不上传：不带你的账号、设备标识或游戏进度。和访问任何网站一样，GitHub 作为托管服务商可能会记录请求的 IP 地址和浏览器 / 系统信息（User-Agent），这由 GitHub 按其隐私声明处理；我们看不到这些记录，也不会用它们来识别你。

如果你的手机开启了 Android 系统备份，系统可能会把本应用的数据（包括游戏进度）备份到你自己的 Google 账号，这由 Google 按其政策管理。

2. 广告（Google AdMob）

游戏里有横幅广告：小镇界面底部和每局打完的结算页底部各有一条小横幅；不弹全屏广告，战斗中没有广告。另外有两处可选的激励视频广告，只有你自己点“看广告”时才会播放：一处在结算页，看完后这一局的碎片多拿 30%；另一处是小镇的每日补给，看完后领一份碎片补给。不看广告不影响正常游玩和进度，游戏也不会自动弹出这类广告。广告由 Google AdMob 提供。为了投放广告、统计广告效果和防止作弊，Google 移动广告 SDK 会自动收集并发送给 Google 以下信息：

  • 设备的 IP 地址（可能用于估算大致位置）；
  • 广告相关的使用情况，例如应用启动、点击、广告观看；
  • 诊断信息，例如应用和 SDK 的性能数据；
  • 设备标识符，例如 Android 广告 ID、应用组 ID。

这些数据在传输过程中经过加密，由 Google 按其隐私政策处理：https://policies.google.com/privacy，Google 如何使用合作伙伴应用中的数据：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites。

你可以在手机的 Android 设置里重置或删除广告 ID，也可以选择不接收个性化广告。

3. Google Play 游戏（排行榜）

游戏里有“守夜榜”和“每周挑战榜”两个排行榜，由 Google Play 游戏服务提供。如果你的手机登录了 Google Play 游戏，打开游戏时会自动登录（不需要另外注册）；没有 Play 游戏账号也可以照常玩，只是不上排行榜。登录后，游戏会把下面这些交给 Google Play 游戏：

  • 排行榜成绩：五个街区守到的最高夜数之和、每周挑战的成绩；
  • 你的 Play 游戏资料（昵称、头像、玩家 ID）会显示在排行榜上，谁能看到由你在 Play 游戏里的资料公开范围设置决定；
  • Google Play 游戏服务 SDK 自己收集的使用情况和诊断信息，用于改进它的稳定性。

这些数据由 Google 按其隐私政策处理（https://policies.google.com/privacy），传输过程中经过加密；我们看不到你的 Google 账号信息。你可以在 Play 游戏的资料设置里修改公开范围，或删除你的 Play 游戏资料和其中的游戏数据。

4. 应用内购买

游戏里用真钱能买的是“钻石”（五档）、“灯火月卡”（买下立刻得钻石和 30 天 VIP，30 天内每天登录再领一份钻石）、“灯塔基金”（每个存档只能买一次，任一街区守到指定夜数分档领钻石），以及节日期间限时出现的小礼包（例如圣诞周的“圣诞小袜”，送灯愿券和节日代币，限购一次）。这些都是一次性购买，不是自动续费的订阅。商城里的其他东西都用钻石买：“幸运盒”（飞机幸运盒、怪物时装幸运盒，开出飞机或影怪的外观，以换样子为主，同一套三件都穿上出战有一点小加成；购买前在商城里能看到每一件开到的机会）、“复活牌”（灯火全灭时用来复活）、“VIP 月卡”（30 天内村民帮忙、每天领补给）等。

“华丽祈愿”是随机抽取：每次用一张“灯愿券”（券不够时可以用钻石代付，一张 30 颗），随机得到华丽飞机皮肤、专属光效、灯芯、星屑、钻石、碎片等。每一档的机会、保底规则（每个祈愿池各自计数，连续 59 次没抽到典藏，第 60 次一定是典藏）和含保底的综合机会，都写在祈愿页的“概率公示”里，抽之前就能看到。付款完全由 Google Play 处理：你的付款方式、银行卡、账单地址等信息只在 Google Play 那里，我们收不到也看不到。

付款成功后，Google Play 会给这个应用一张购买凭证（一串随机代码），游戏只用它来发放你买到的东西（钻石、灯火月卡、灯塔基金或礼包里的道具），并确保同一笔购买只发一次；凭证和你得到的东西记在你的游戏进度里。退款按 Google Play 的退款政策处理（https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637）。

5. 云端存档（Google Play 游戏）

为了让你换手机、重装游戏后还能找回进度和买到的东西，如果你登录了 Google Play 游戏，游戏会把整份游戏进度（街区、碎片、机库、称号、拥有的皮肤和时装、钻石、复活牌、VIP 月卡和灯火月卡的有效期、灯塔基金买过没有和领过哪几档、处理过的购买凭证等）存一份到 Google Play 游戏的“已保存的游戏”（存在你自己的 Google 账号里）。这里面不包含你的个人信息；我们看不到。你可以在 Play 游戏的资料设置里删除这个游戏的数据。没有登录 Play 游戏时，这些只保存在手机上。

6. 游戏统计（Google Analytics for Firebase）

为了知道游戏哪里太难、哪里不好玩，游戏会把你在游戏里的操作记成统计数据，发给 Google Analytics for Firebase：例如守了哪个街区第几夜、赢了还是输了、用了几次复活、开放了哪个街区、在商城里买了什么、钻石和碎片是怎么得到和用掉的，以及游戏语言、是否 VIP 这类设置。你用真钱购买时，还会记录买的是哪件商品、结果（成功、等待付款、取消或失败）、有没有买过真钱商品，以及 Google Play 返回的价格和币种（Firebase 自动记录的“应用内购买”事件）；不记录付款方式、银行卡和购买凭证。同时会记录 Firebase 自动生成的应用实例 ID（重装游戏就会换一个新的）、手机型号、系统版本、应用版本、大致国家 / 地区（由 IP 推算，不保存 IP）和使用时长。

游戏统计不收集你的名字、邮箱、广告 ID、Android ID（广告 ID 只用于第 2 条的 Google 广告）；这些统计只用来看整体的数字（例如多少人守住了第 10 夜），不用来识别你是谁，不用于投放或个性化广告，也不会卖给任何人。数据由 Google 按其隐私政策处理（https://policies.google.com/privacy），传输过程中经过加密，保留期限按 Google Analytics 的设置（最长 14 个月），到期自动删除。

7. 欧洲等地区的广告同意

在法律要求征得同意的地区（例如欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士），游戏会通过 Google 的用户消息平台（UMP）询问你是否同意个性化广告。你可以随时在开始画面或小镇顶栏的“隐私设置”里修改这个选择。

8. 儿童

本游戏不以 13 岁以下儿童为目标用户。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

The Last Lamp — Privacy Policy

Developer: 森澜岛游戏 · App: The Last Lamp (Android, package com.lastlamp.game) · Effective date: November 16, 2026 (version 6.7)

1. We have no accounts or servers of our own

The Last Lamp is a single-player game with no accounts and no servers of our own (leaderboards and cloud save use Google Play Games, see sections 3 and 5; purchases are handled by Google Play, see section 4). Your progress (repaired houses, lit lamps, shards, plane level and so on) and your sound setting are stored inside the app on your phone; if you are signed in to Google Play Games, your progress is also saved to the cloud in your own Google account (see section 5). We cannot see them and they are never uploaded to us. To improve the game, the app sends gameplay statistics, without your name or email, to Google Analytics for Firebase (see section 6).

If you tap "Share" in the game, the game creates a picture of your town and a short text with a link on your phone and hands them to the app you choose (for example Facebook or WhatsApp); you decide whether and to whom to send them. The shared content contains only game visuals and progress, no personal information, and is then handled by that app under its own privacy policy. Our share pages (nickooi.github.io/lastlamp/share/) only show the link preview and redirect to Google Play; they do not track visits or show ads.

The game downloads content updates and feature switches (for example, whether a feature such as Wishes is temporarily turned off) from our pages on GitHub Pages (nickooi.github.io). These requests only download data and upload nothing: they carry no account, device identifier or game progress. As with visiting any website, GitHub, as the hosting provider, may log the IP address and browser / system information (User-Agent) of each request, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We cannot see these logs and never use them to identify you.

If Android system backup is turned on, Android may back up this app's data (including your progress) to your own Google account, under Google's policies.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads: a small banner at the bottom of the town screen and of the results screen after each battle. There are no pop-up full-screen ads and no ads during battles. There are also two optional rewarded video ads, which play only when you choose to tap "Watch ad": one on the results screen, where watching gives you 30% more shards for that run, and one for the town's daily supply, where watching gives you a supply of shards. Not watching ads does not affect normal play or progress, and these ads never appear automatically. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate general location);
  • ad-related interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information such as app and SDK performance;
  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit and handled by Google under its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy; how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Android settings.

3. Google Play Games (leaderboards)

The game has two leaderboards, "Night Watch" and "Weekly Challenge", provided by Google Play Games services. If your phone is signed in to Google Play Games, the game signs you in automatically when it starts (no separate registration); without a Play Games account you can still play, you just won't appear on the leaderboards. Once signed in, the game sends Google Play Games:

  • leaderboard scores: the sum of the highest night reached in the five districts, and your Weekly Challenge score;
  • your Play Games profile (gamer name, avatar, player ID) is shown on the leaderboards; who can see it depends on your Play Games profile visibility setting;
  • usage and diagnostic data collected by the Google Play Games services SDK itself to improve its stability.

This data is handled by Google under its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and encrypted in transit; we cannot see your Google account information. You can change your profile visibility, or delete your Play Games profile and its game data, in your Play Games profile settings.

4. In-app purchases

Real money buys Diamonds (five packs), the Lamplight Monthly Card (Diamonds and 30 days of VIP right away, plus Diamonds every day you log in for 30 days), the Lighthouse Fund (once per save; Diamonds in tiers as you reach set nights in any district) and small limited-time holiday packs that appear only during events (for example the "Little Stocking" during Christmas Week, which gives Wish Tickets and event tokens and can be bought once). These are all one-time purchases, not auto-renewing subscriptions. Everything else in the shop is bought with Diamonds, including Lucky Boxes (Plane Lucky Box and Monster Costume Box, which give looks for your plane or the shadows; they are mostly about looks, and wearing a full set of three gives a small bonus in battle; the chance of each piece is shown in the shop before you buy), Revive Cards (used to revive when the lamps go out) and the VIP Pass (30 days of a villager helper and daily supplies). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: your payment method, card and billing address stay with Google Play, and we never receive or see them.

"Spectacular Wishes" is a random draw: each draw uses one Wish Ticket (if you are short, you can pay with Diamonds instead, 30 per ticket) and gives a random reward such as a grand plane skin, exclusive light effects, wicks, stardust, Diamonds or shards. The chance of each tier, the pity rule (each wish pool counts separately; if you go 59 draws without the top prize, the 60th draw is guaranteed to be it) and the overall chance including pity are shown under "Odds" on the wish page before you draw.

After a successful payment, Google Play gives the app a purchase token (a random code). The game uses it only to deliver what you bought (Diamonds, the Monthly Card, the Lighthouse Fund or the items in a pack) and to make sure each purchase is delivered exactly once; the token and everything you receive are kept in your game progress. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

5. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

So that you get your progress and purchases back on a new phone or after reinstalling, if you are signed in to Google Play Games the game saves a copy of your whole game progress (districts, shards, hangar, titles, owned skins and costumes, Diamonds, Revive Cards, VIP Pass and Monthly Card expiry, whether you bought the Lighthouse Fund and which payouts you claimed, processed purchase tokens and so on) to Google Play Games Saved Games (stored in your own Google account). It contains no personal information, and we cannot see it. You can delete this game's data in your Play Games profile settings. If you are not signed in to Play Games, this is kept only on your phone.

6. Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To find out where the game is too hard or not fun, the app records what you do in the game as statistics and sends them to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example which district and night you defended, whether you won or lost, how many times you revived, which districts you unlocked, what you bought in the shop, how you earned and spent Diamonds and shards, and settings such as the game language and whether you are a VIP. When you buy something with real money, it also records which item it was, the result (purchased, pending, cancelled or failed), whether you have ever bought anything with real money, and the price and currency returned by Google Play (the "in-app purchase" event Firebase records automatically); it does not record your payment method, card or purchase token. It also records an app instance ID generated by Firebase (a new one after you reinstall), your phone model, OS version, app version, approximate country / region (estimated from the IP address, which is not stored) and how long you play.

Gameplay statistics do not include your name, email, advertising ID or Android ID (the advertising ID is used only for Google ads, see section 2). These statistics are only used to look at overall numbers (for example how many players got through night 10), never to identify you, never for serving or personalizing ads, and are never sold. The data is handled by Google under its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), encrypted in transit, and kept for the period set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months), after which it is deleted automatically.

7. Ad consent in Europe and other regions

Where consent is required by law (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent to personalized ads through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your choice at any time from “Privacy” (“隐私设置” in Chinese) on the title screen or in the town's top bar.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data Deletion Requests

This section applies to the game "最后一盏灯 (The Last Lamp)" on Google Play (developer: 森澜岛游戏).

How to request: Email perngn88@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request - The Last Lamp". Include your Google Play Games player name (if any) and which data you want deleted. We will process your request and reply within 30 days.

You can also delete some data yourself right away:

  • On-device game data: uninstall the game, or clear the app's data in Android settings, to delete progress and settings stored on your phone.
  • Cloud saves, leaderboard scores and your Play Games profile: open the Google Play Games app and delete this game's data in its settings, as described in sections 3 and 5.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Data that gets deleted: on-device progress and settings, cloud saves, leaderboard scores and your Play Games profile (using the steps above; if you email us, we will walk you through each step).

Data we keep:

  • Purchase and order records: kept by Google Play under Google's policies and legal requirements (such as tax and refunds). We cannot delete them. You can view them in Google Play under "Payments & subscriptions".
  • Ad-related data: handled by Google AdMob under Google's privacy policy. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Game analytics data: not linked to your name, email or player name, so we cannot find and delete it for a single player; it is deleted automatically when the analytics retention period ends (at most 14 months).

How purchase records are used: In-game purchases are processed by Google Play billing, and we cannot see your payment details. Our analytics only records which item was bought, to help us understand how the game is doing.
